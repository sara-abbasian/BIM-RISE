# BIM-RISE
BIM-driven decision-support framework for construction and demolition waste estimation, material recovery planning, and environmental impact assessment.
## Project Overview

BIM-RISE is a Building Information Modelling–based decision-support framework developed to improve the planning and management of construction and demolition materials. The project addresses a common challenge in the construction industry: decisions about material reuse, recycling, transportation, and disposal are often made too late, when opportunities to reduce waste, cost, and environmental impact have already been limited.

The framework uses information extracted from BIM models, including building elements, material types, quantities, and volumes, to estimate the amount and composition of construction and demolition waste associated with a project. Materials can then be classified according to their potential for reuse, recycling, recovery, or disposal.

BIM-RISE transforms technical BIM and environmental data into practical indicators that can support contractors, designers, project managers, owners, waste-management professionals, and sustainability teams. The framework allows users to compare different material-management scenarios and understand how each scenario may affect waste diversion, transportation requirements, project costs, energy consumption, and carbon emissions.

The results are presented through interactive Power BI dashboards, allowing project stakeholders to explore material quantities, waste categories, recovery opportunities, and environmental impacts in a clear and accessible format. By integrating BIM-based quantity information with sustainability assessment and data visualization, BIM-RISE supports more informed, transparent, and data-driven decision-making throughout the project lifecycle.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of BIM-RISE are to:

* Extract material and quantity information directly from BIM models.
* Estimate construction and demolition waste volumes at an earlier project stage.
* Classify materials based on their potential for reuse, recycling, recovery, or landfill disposal.
* Compare alternative waste-management and material-recovery scenarios.
* Evaluate environmental indicators, including energy consumption and carbon emissions.
* Support transportation, cost, and material-flow planning.
* Present complex project information through interactive Power BI dashboards.
* Improve communication and coordination among project stakeholders.
* Support circular-economy strategies in the architecture, engineering, and construction industry.

## Problem Addressed

Construction and demolition activities generate large quantities of material waste. However, project teams may not have access to accurate, organized, and easily understandable information about material quantities until construction or demolition is already underway.

Traditional waste-planning approaches can depend on manual calculations, disconnected spreadsheets, general assumptions, and late-stage site assessments. These limitations can make it difficult to identify reusable materials, estimate recycling opportunities, compare disposal alternatives, or evaluate the environmental consequences of different decisions.

BIM-RISE addresses this problem by connecting BIM-based material information with waste estimation, sustainability assessment, scenario comparison, and dashboard visualization.

## Proposed Solution

The BIM-RISE framework provides a structured workflow in which:

1. Building elements and material quantities are extracted from a BIM model.
2. Materials are organized into relevant construction and demolition waste categories.
3. Waste quantities and material-recovery potential are estimated.
4. Reuse, recycling, recovery, and disposal scenarios are developed.
5. Cost, transportation, energy, and carbon-emission indicators are calculated.
6. Results are visualized in interactive Power BI dashboards.
7. Stakeholders compare scenarios and use the results to support project planning and decision-making.

BIM-RISE can help project teams:

* Reduce construction and demolition waste sent to landfill.
* Improve the accuracy of material and waste estimates.
* Compare the environmental effects of different management strategies.
* Support reuse, recycling, and circular-economy planning.

## Project Significance

BIM-RISE demonstrates how BIM can be used for more than design coordination and quantity take-off. It shows how digital building information can support sustainability, circular-economy planning, material recovery, environmental assessment, and strategic project decision-making.

The project contributes to the digital transformation of the architecture, engineering, and construction industry by connecting BIM, environmental data, analytics, and visualization within one integrated decision-support workflow.
